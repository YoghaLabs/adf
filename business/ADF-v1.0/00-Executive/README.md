**Phase:** BUSINESS-002

## Purpose

The Executive Package explains ADF at leadership level: what it is, why it exists,
who it serves, why the market needs it, and the long-term and founder vision.

**Audience:** investors, enterprise customers, government stakeholders, partners,
and internal management.

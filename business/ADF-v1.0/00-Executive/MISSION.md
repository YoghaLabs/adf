# Mission

## Objective

Define ADF’s mission across product, technology, community, enterprise, and
education so stakeholders share one purpose statement.

## Background

Mission converts vision into ongoing obligations. ADF’s mission must reflect
shipped principles: documentation-first, architecture discipline, service-driven
design, and human decision authority.

## Analysis

Without an explicit mission, teams over-index on features and under-invest in
continuity, governance, and education. ADF’s differentiator depends on all five
mission domains working together.

## Strategy

Operate under one mission:

> Enable reliable, auditable, AI-assisted software engineering by making
> documentation, architecture, and collaboration first-class—across product,
> technology, community, enterprise, and education.

## Recommendations

### Product
Ship coherent surfaces (Studio, Core, docs) that reinforce continuity and clarity.

### Technology
Preserve Service Layer boundaries, locked architecture, and presentation-only Studio.

### Community
Share practices and contracts that make multi-session AI work resumable and reviewable.

### Enterprise
Support governance, identity, audit narratives, and adoption paths suitable for
organizational standards.

### Education
Teach documentation-first and architecture-first habits—not only prompt tricks.

## Summary

ADF’s mission is to make AI-assisted engineering trustworthy and continuous by
uniting product capability, technical discipline, community practice, enterprise
readiness, and education—without replacing human judgment.

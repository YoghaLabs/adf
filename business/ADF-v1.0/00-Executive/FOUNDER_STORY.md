## Analysis

Building with AI assistants surfaces a recurring operational problem: speed
without memory. Experienced architecture practice shows that systems fail when
decisions, boundaries, and documentation are not treated as product assets.

The founder’s response was to design ADF as an **operating system for
AI-assisted development**—contracts, state, knowledge, and controlled presentation—
rather than another isolated generator.

## Strategy

cumulative builds, explicit stop rules, and clear separation between engineering
SSOT, legal package, and business strategy package.

## Recommendations

## Summary

Yogha Prayoto created ADF to address context loss and architectural drift in
AI-assisted engineering. The founder vision prioritizes durable structure,
human decision authority, and enterprise-ready discipline over hype-driven
automation.

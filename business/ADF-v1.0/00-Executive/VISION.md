# Vision

## Objective

State ADF’s long-term vision in language suitable for investors, enterprises,
government stakeholders, and partners.

## Background

ADF began as a response to unreliable AI-assisted development continuity. The
product has matured into a Release Candidate with Core, Studio, and governance
surfaces. Vision must remain ambitious yet grounded in what the architecture
allows.

## Analysis

Enterprises will not standardize on tools that treat AI as disposable chat.
They need platforms that preserve architecture, decisions, and accountability
across people and agents. The durable category is not “better autocomplete”—it
is **AI development infrastructure**.

## Strategy

Pursue the vision of ADF as **the AI Development Platform for enterprise
software engineering**: a system where documentation, architecture, collaboration,
orchestration planning, and governance reinforce each other.

## Recommendations

1. Anchor all external messaging to this vision statement.
2. Separate vision from near-term roadmap (see `LONG_TERM_VISION.md`).
3. Reject positioning that reduces ADF to chatbot, generator, or IDE clones.

## Summary

**Vision:** ADF becomes the AI Development Platform for enterprise software
engineering—where humans and AI participants build on shared, durable project
truth rather than ephemeral sessions.

# Why ADF

## Objective

Give a simple, executive-ready answer to: why should someone adopt ADF?

## Background

Buyers compare many AI tools. Adoption needs a clear reason beyond feature lists.

## Analysis

Teams adopt ADF when they need **continuity and structure** as much as speed:

- Shared truth in the repository
- Resumable AI and human work
- Architecture that does not drift by accident
- Collaboration and governance models that fit organizations
- A control center (Studio) that presents services—not hidden business logic

If the need is only quick snippets, lighter tools may suffice. If the need is
reliable AI-assisted delivery, ADF is the better fit.

## Strategy

Explain adoption as risk reduction and clarity: less context loss, clearer
process, stronger alignment between documentation, engineering, and business.

## Recommendations

1. Lead with “why ADF” before deep feature tours.
2. Pair this document with `ONE_PAGE_EXECUTIVE.md` for short meetings.
3. Qualify prospects: prefer teams that value process integrity.

## Summary

Adopt ADF to make AI-assisted software engineering continuous, structured, and
governable—so humans and AI participants build on the same durable project truth.

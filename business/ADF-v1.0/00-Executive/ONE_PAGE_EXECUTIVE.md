## Analysis

AI coding tools accelerate output but often lose context, weaken documentation,
and fragment process knowledge. Enterprises need continuity, architecture
discipline, and governance—not only generation speed.

## Strategy

ADF keeps contracts, state, knowledge, collaboration, and control-center UX in a
locked, repository-native architecture (Core + Studio + docs + legal + business
SSOT). AI is a participant; humans remain decision makers. v1 emphasizes planning
and governance models—not unsupervised autonomy.

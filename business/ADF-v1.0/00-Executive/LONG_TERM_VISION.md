# Long-Term Vision

## Objective

Describe a realistic 3-, 5-, and 10-year horizon for ADF without unrealistic
promises.

## Background

ADF `1.0.0-rc1` establishes the engineering foundation. Long-term vision must
extend from that base: platform maturity, enterprise adoption, and disciplined
automation—not sudden autonomy claims.

## Analysis

Over-promising autonomy or market dominance undermines trust. Under-describing
direction leaves investors and partners without a path. The balance is staged
maturity.

## Strategy

### 3 years
- Stabilize v1 line (GA quality gates, packaging, adoption playbooks)
- Deepen enterprise governance and partner integrations
- Expand documentation and education for real delivery teams

### 5 years
- Broader enterprise footprint as a standard AI engineering platform layer
- Richer orchestration and participant workflows with explicit human control
- Stronger ecosystem (packages, templates, marketplace maturity)

### 10 years
- Recognized category reference for structured AI software engineering
- Durable architecture still coherent with founding principles
- Optional advanced automation only where governance and product readiness allow

## Recommendations

1. Use this horizon in investor conversations; keep numbers and dates qualitative.
2. Revisit horizons after BUSINESS-004/005 with evidence—not hype.
3. Never imply unsupervised multi-agent production control as a near-term guarantee.

## Summary

ADF’s long-term vision is steady platform maturity: solidify v1, earn enterprise
trust, grow ecosystem and controlled workflows—while preserving human decision
authority and architectural integrity.

# Market Opportunity

## Objective

Describe high-level market opportunity drivers for ADF without numeric market
sizing estimates.

## Background

Demand for AI in software delivery is rising across enterprises and public-sector
digitization programs. Buyers evaluate productivity tools and, increasingly,
platforms that reduce operational risk.

## Analysis

### AI adoption
Organizations are moving from experiments to broader developer use of AI
assistants. Adoption increases the cost of poor continuity and weak governance.

### Enterprise software
Enterprises need standards, auditability, and role clarity. Tools that cannot
explain process struggle in procurement and security review.

### Developer productivity
Productivity pressure remains high. Gains that create cleanup work later are
less valuable than gains that preserve structure.

### Knowledge management
As AI accelerates change, knowledge systems must keep pace. Repository-native
memory becomes strategic.

## Strategy

Position ADF at the intersection of AI-assisted engineering and enterprise
knowledge/process discipline—without claiming a quantified TAM in this document.

## Recommendations

1. Keep BUSINESS-002 qualitative; defer quantified sizing to Market Analysis (BUSINESS-004).
2. Emphasize trend alignment: adoption, enterprise rigor, productivity with structure, knowledge durability.
3. Avoid speculative forecasts.

## Summary

Market opportunity for ADF is driven by AI adoption, enterprise software
requirements, productivity expectations, and knowledge-management pressure—not
by a single tool category fad.

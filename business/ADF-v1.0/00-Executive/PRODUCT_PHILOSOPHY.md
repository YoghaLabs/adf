# Product Philosophy

## Objective

Document the principles that govern how ADF is designed and explained.

## Background

Philosophy prevents feature sprawl and messaging drift. ADF’s engineering ADRs
already encode many of these ideas; this document states them for business
audiences.

## Analysis

Teams fail when AI output outruns shared understanding. Philosophy must put
documentation and architecture ahead of opportunistic generation, and keep
humans accountable for decisions.

## Strategy

Operate under these principles:

1. **Documentation First** — useful docs precede and constrain delivery.
2. **Architecture First** — locked structure and ADRs before expansion.
3. **Business follows Product** — commercial narrative matches shipped capability.
4. **AI follows Documentation** — agents operate against contracts and SSOT, not guesswork.
5. **Human remains decision maker** — AI participates; humans approve consequential choices.

## Recommendations

1. Use these principles in sales and investor Q&A.
2. Reject proposals that invert the order (e.g., autonomous execution before governance readiness).
3. Keep philosophy language stable across BUSINESS phases.

## Summary

ADF’s product philosophy prioritizes documentation, architecture, product-truthful
business messaging, documentation-guided AI, and human decision authority.

# Brand Positioning

## Objective

Position ADF clearly in the market: what it is, and what it is not.

## Background

Category confusion destroys enterprise deals. ADF must not be mistaken for a
chatbot, a one-shot code generator, or a general-purpose IDE.

## Analysis

Adjacent categories:

- **Chatbots** optimize conversation, not repository continuity.
- **Code generators** optimize output volume, not operating contracts.
- **IDEs** optimize editing environments; ADF Studio is a control center, not an IDE replacement.

ADF’s center of gravity is framework + platform discipline for AI engineering.

## Strategy

Position ADF as:

- **AI Development Framework**
- **Enterprise AI Engineering Platform** (direction and readiness narrative)

Explicitly **not**:

- Chatbot
- Code Generator (as the primary identity)
- IDE

## Recommendations

1. Lead every pitch with the two positive position statements above.
2. Include a short “ADF is not…” clarification in executive materials.
3. Align website and sales copy to this document; avoid contradictory slogans.

## Summary

ADF is positioned as an AI Development Framework and enterprise AI engineering
platform—not a chatbot, not a generator-first product, and not an IDE.

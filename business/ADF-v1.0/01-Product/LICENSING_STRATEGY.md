# Licensing Strategy

## Objective

Explain licensing philosophy by segment. This is strategy only — not legal clauses.

## Background

Product legal texts live in `/legal` (Community License, EULA, copyright, trademark).
Commercial use requires permission from the copyright holder. This document guides
how editions relate to licensing posture.

## Current Situation

- Community Edition: non-commercial grant under ADF Community License.
- Professional / Enterprise: commercial permission / agreements (to be operationalized).
- Education: typically aligned with Community non-commercial use unless a separate
  education program is later defined.
- Partners: commercial/partner terms as separately agreed.
- Cloud: future service terms — not available now.

## Analysis

Licensing must protect the creator’s rights, keep Community usable for learning and
evaluation, and give organizations a clear commercial path. Strategy must not rewrite
`/legal`.

## Strategy

| Segment | Licensing philosophy |
|---------|----------------------|
| **Community** | Broad enough for learning, research, evaluation, non-commercial development; commercial use not included by default |
| **Commercial (Professional)** | Explicit permission / commercial license for business use and delivery teams |
| **Enterprise** | Enterprise agreement covering organizational deployment, governance expectations, and support commitments |
| **Partner** | Written partner terms for redistribution, integration, or delivery on behalf of customers |
| **Education** | Prefer Community pathway for teaching and research; specialized education terms only if later established |

Principles:

1. `/legal` remains authoritative for grant language.
2. Business documents describe approach; they do not substitute for license text.
3. Edition names in sales must match the legal posture offered.

## Recommendations

1. Point all license questions to `/legal` first.
2. Do not invent open-source claims if the Community License is rights-reserved.
3. Record any future license model change as a BD decision before marketing it.

## Implementation Plan

BUSINESS legal/operations phases and counsel operationalize agreements; product strategy stays aligned.

## Deliverables

Segment licensing philosophy for GTM consistency.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Strategy text conflicts with `/legal` | Conflict rule: `/legal` wins |

## Success Metrics

Sales and partner drafts cite editions without contradicting Community License facts.

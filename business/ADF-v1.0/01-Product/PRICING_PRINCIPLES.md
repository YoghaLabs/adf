# Pricing Principles

## Objective

Define pricing philosophy for ADF commercial offerings. **No prices or numeric estimates.**

## Background

Editions and licensing strategy establish who pays and why. Pricing numbers belong
in later commercial design (Business Model / Sales), not in this principles document.

## Current Situation

Community is positioned for non-commercial use under `/legal`. Professional and
Enterprise are commercial strategies. Cloud is future vision. No price list is
published in this package.

## Analysis

Enterprise and government buyers distrust opaque or volatile pricing. Principles must
favor clarity and predictability before any number is set.

## Strategy

Adopt these principles for all future price-setting work:

| Principle | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Value-based** | Price reflects organizational value (continuity, governance, support), not arbitrary feature locks |
| **Transparent** | Published commercial terms should be understandable; avoid hidden mandatory add-ons by surprise |
| **Predictable** | Prefer structures organizations can budget (stable edition rungs; clear upgrade rules) |
| **Enterprise-friendly** | Support procurement needs: clear edition scope, support expectations, deployment options |
| **Honest packaging** | Do not charge for “features” that are not shipped; roadmap items stay roadmap |
| **Community respect** | Community remains useful; pricing pressure must not hollow the evaluation/learning path |

## Recommendations

1. Set numbers only in Business Model / Sales phases, citing these principles.
2. Never put prices in Executive or Product Strategy docs without a BD update.
3. Keep upgrade economics aligned with `UPGRADE_PATH.md`.

# Product Positioning

## Objective

Position ADF clearly for commercial and institutional audiences.

## Background

Executive Package (BUSINESS-002) positioned ADF as an AI Development Framework and
enterprise AI engineering platform. Product Strategy must keep that identity when
defining editions and packaging.

## Current Situation

ADF `1.0.0-rc1` is a framework + Studio control-center stack with repository-native
SSOT, collaboration, planning orchestration, and governance foundations. It is not
a chatbot product, a coding-assistant chat UI, a general IDE, or a low-code app builder.

## Analysis

Category confusion causes failed evaluations. Buyers comparing ADF to chat tools or
IDEs will score the wrong attributes. Positioning must state both inclusion and
exclusion.

## Strategy

### ADF is

- **AI Development Framework**
- **Enterprise AI Engineering Platform** (direction; enterprise packaging builds on RC1 foundations)

### ADF is not

- A chatbot
- An AI coding assistant (as primary product identity)
- An IDE
- A low-code / no-code application platform

Studio is a **control center** over services and Core — not an editor replacement.

## Recommendations

1. Lead product and sales materials with the two positive statements above.
2. Include a short “ADF is not…” block in edition and investor briefs.
3. Do not rebrand ADF as a chat or low-code product in later BUSINESS phases.

## Implementation Plan

Align Sales, Marketing, and Investor packages to this positioning without rename.

## Deliverables

Consistent positioning language across `01-Product/` and downstream phases.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Sales language drifts to “AI assistant” | Enforce this document + BD-007 |

## Success Metrics

External materials pass a category test: removing the ADF name still sounds like a
framework/platform, not a chat IDE.

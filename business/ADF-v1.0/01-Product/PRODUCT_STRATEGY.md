# Product Strategy

## Objective

Define how ADF becomes a commercial software product and establish this document
set as the SSOT for subsequent product decisions.

## Background

ADF engineering through BUILD-020 delivered `1.0.0-rc1` (Production Release
Candidate): engines, distribution, Studio, collaboration, orchestration
(planning), and enterprise governance foundations. Legal Community License is
published under `/legal`. BUSINESS-002 fixed executive positioning.

## Current Situation

- Product capability exists as one RC1 codebase and documentation set.
- Commercial editions (Professional, Enterprise, Cloud) are **product strategy**,
  not separate shipped SKUs at RC1.
- External audiences need a clear path from Community use to commercial offerings
  without overstating what is live today.

## Analysis

Without a product strategy SSOT, later Market, Pricing, Sales, and Investor work
will invent inconsistent edition and roadmap stories. Strategy must separate:

1. **Shipped (RC1)** — what BUILD-001…020 actually delivered  
2. **Edition packaging** — how commercial offerings will be composed  
3. **Roadmap themes** — intentional future direction (not current availability)

## Strategy

1. **Vision:** ADF as the AI Development Framework and enterprise AI engineering
   platform for structured, documentation-first delivery.
2. **Goals:** Useful Community Edition; clear Professional path for software houses
   and consultants; Enterprise focus on governance, audit, deployment, and support;
   Cloud as future vision only.
3. **Target users:** Developers, architects, technical leads, AI-assisted delivery teams.
4. **Target organizations:** Software companies, system integrators, enterprises,
   government/institutions, educational bodies (evaluation and learning).
5. **Enterprise focus:** Continuity, governance models, auditability, controlled
   collaboration — not unsupervised autonomy in v1.
6. **Community ecosystem:** Templates, marketplace packages, documentation, and
   learning pathways that keep Community genuinely usable.
7. **Long-term sustainability:** Business follows Product; licensing and packaging
   fund maintenance without hollowing out Community.

## Recommendations

1. Treat this folder as product decision SSOT for BUSINESS-004 onward.
2. Label every forward-looking claim as roadmap or edition packaging intent.
3. Keep Cloud messaging explicitly future-facing.

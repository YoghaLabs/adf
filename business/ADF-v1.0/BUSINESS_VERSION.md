# Business Version

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Business package version | `B1.0.0` |
| Product version aligned | `1.0.0-rc1` |
| Active BUSINESS phase | BUSINESS-001 (complete) |
| Next BUSINESS phase | BUSINESS-002 |
| Structure revision | `locked-v1` |

## Versioning Policy

- Business package versions use `BMAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`.
- `B1.0.0` marks foundation lock for ADF v1.0 business work.
- Phase completions are recorded in `BUSINESS_CHANGELOG.md` and `BUSINESS_HISTORY.md`.
- Product semver remains root `VERSION` and must not be silently rewritten by business phases.

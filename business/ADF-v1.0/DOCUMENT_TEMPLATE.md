# Document Template

**Mandatory template for all future business strategy documents in `business/ADF-v1.0/`.**

Copy this structure. Keep section headings stable so packages remain comparable across phases.

---

# Title

## Objective

What this document must achieve.

## Background

Context that a new reader needs before the analysis.

## Current Situation

Facts about ADF, market, customers, or internal state **as of the document date**.

## Analysis

Evidence-based assessment. Separate facts from interpretation.

## Strategy

Chosen approach and why it is preferred.

## Recommendations

Concrete recommendations for decision-makers.

## Implementation Plan

Phased actions, owners (roles), and dependencies.

## Deliverables

Tangible outputs this document commits to (Markdown masters and any generated artifacts).

## Risks

Key risks, likelihood/impact (qualitative), and mitigations.

## Success Metrics

How success will be measured.

## Next Actions

Immediate next steps and the BUSINESS phase they belong to.

# SWOT Analysis

## Objective

Provide a SWOT based only on ADF `1.0.0-rc1` reality and locked business positioning.

## Background

SWOT informs Business Model and Investor narratives. It must not invent market sizes
or unshipped strengths.

## Current Situation

Strengths and weaknesses reflect BUILD-001…020 delivery and known RC1 exclusions.
Opportunities and threats are qualitative market conditions.

## Analysis

RC1 is a coherent foundation with clear limits. Overstating strengths creates
enterprise distrust; ignoring weaknesses blocks prioritization.

## Strategy

### Strengths (RC1)

- Documentation-first, architecture-locked repository model
- Cumulative Core engines + Studio control center + SDK/distribution foundations
- AI collaboration model (participant, not uncontrolled autonomy claim)
- Planning-oriented orchestration with human decision authority
- Enterprise governance foundations present in architecture
- Business + Engineering + Legal SSOT separation already established
- Clear product positioning (framework/platform — not chatbot/IDE/low-code)

### Weaknesses (RC1)

- Release Candidate — not GA; signing/coverage gates remain
- Live production IdP SSO wiring out of RC1
- No autonomous multi-agent execution (may disappoint autonomy seekers)
- Cloud Edition not available
- Commercial edition SKUs are strategy, not separate mature product lines yet
- Adoption requires process change (higher friction than a plugin)

### Opportunities

- Organizations seeking structure after early AI-tool experimentation
- Software houses / SIs needing repeatable AI-assisted delivery
- Enterprise/government demand for auditability and documentation discipline
- Platform-engineering alignment for internal standards
- Education and Community as durable top-of-funnel without crippleware

### Threats

- Category mis-comparison against AI coding assistants on chat metrics alone
- Buyer expectation inflation (autonomy, Cloud, instant SSO)
- Tooling fatigue / preference for status-quo assistant + wiki stacks
- License misunderstanding (Community vs commercial use)
- Narrative drift in later GTM phases undoing RC1 honesty

## Recommendations

1. Use Strengths in positioning; use Weaknesses in discovery honesty.
2. Convert Opportunities into P1 target arenas.
3. Track Threats in `MARKET_RISKS.md` with owners in later phases.

## Implementation Plan

Business Model addresses packaging/commercialization of strengths; roadmap themes address weaknesses over time.

## Deliverables

RC1-grounded SWOT for diligence.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| SWOT used as marketing brochure | Keep weakness section mandatory in briefings |

## Success Metrics

Investor/enterprise Q&A can recite at least two weaknesses unprompted.

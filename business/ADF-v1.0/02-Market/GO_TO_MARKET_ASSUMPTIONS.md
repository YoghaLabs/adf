# Go-To-Market Assumptions

## Objective

State explicit go-to-market assumptions so later phases can test or revise them.

## Background

Assumptions are not facts. They guide Business Model and Sales until evidence
replaces them. No market-size figures are implied.

## Current Situation

ADF has Product Strategy editions and Market SSOT, but commercial motions are not
yet fully operationalized. Assumptions must stay conservative and RC1-aware.

## Analysis

Hidden assumptions cause GTM thrash. Writing them down is a control mechanism.

## Strategy

### Assumed true (working hypotheses)

1. Buyers who felt AI context loss will evaluate frameworks more seriously than chat plugins.
2. Software houses, consultants, and SIs are reachable earlier than large enterprise procurement.
3. Community Edition can be a credible evaluation path without artificial crippling.
4. Professional edition is the primary first commercial landing for delivery businesses.
5. Enterprise deals require governance/audit/deployment narratives and longer cycles.
6. Self-managed deployment is acceptable for P1 segments in the near term.
7. Category education (what ADF is / is not) is mandatory in every serious evaluation.
8. GA completion will improve institutional willingness vs RC1 alone.

### Assumed false / not relied upon

1. Cloud Edition availability in the current GTM window.
2. Unsupervised multi-agent execution as a current selling point.
3. Live production IdP SSO as a current RC1 checkbox.
4. Invented TAM/SAM/SOM as planning inputs.
5. That ADF replaces PM or DevOps platforms outright.

## Recommendations

1. Mark each assumption validated/invalidated after pilots.
2. Do not build pricing or headcount plans on unstated assumptions.
3. Change assumptions only via `BUSINESS_DECISIONS.md` when durable.

## Implementation Plan

Business Model and Sales reference this list; Marketing does not contradict it.

## Deliverables

Assumption register for GTM governance.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Assumptions treated as proven market data | Label as hypotheses |

## Success Metrics

Quarterly (or phase) review updates assumption status with evidence notes.

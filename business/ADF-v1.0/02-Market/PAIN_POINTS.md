# Pain Points

## Objective

Capture the pain points ADF is designed to address, mapped to audiences.

## Background

Executive and Product packages already describe fragmentation, knowledge loss, and
inconsistent AI process. This document is the market-facing pain map.

## Current Situation

Observed pains are operational and architectural. They are not claimed as survey
results with percentages.

## Analysis

Pains cluster around continuity and accountability. Tools that only accelerate
generation can worsen these pains if process discipline is absent.

## Strategy

| Pain point | Who feels it | ADF response (RC1-aligned) |
|------------|--------------|----------------------------|
| Context fragmentation across sessions/tools | Developers, Tech Leads, Managers | Repository-native contracts, state, knowledge |
| Inconsistent AI behavior / unclear operating rules | Tech Leads, Architects | AI as participant under shared contracts |
| Knowledge loss on handoff | Managers, Software Houses | Documented SSOT and resumable work |
| Poor or stale documentation | Architects, Government IT | Documentation-first operating model |
| Vendor/tool dependency without process ownership | CTO, Enterprise | Framework approach; self-managed deployment |
| Disconnected assistants, wikis, tickets, pipelines | Architects, Platform-minded teams | Integrated Core + Studio control center (complements, not replaces, PM/DevOps) |
| Weak governance narrative for AI-assisted work | Enterprise, Government | Governance foundations; hardening on roadmap |

## Recommendations

1. Discovery should confirm at least two pains before deep demo.
2. Do not invent pains ADF cannot address (e.g., full DevOps replacement).
3. Separate “planning orchestration” from “autonomous agents” when discussing AI control.

## Implementation Plan

Sales discovery scripts use this table; Marketing turns pains into problem statements.

|------|------------|

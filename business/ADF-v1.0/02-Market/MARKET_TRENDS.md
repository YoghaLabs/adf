# Market Trends

## Objective

Describe qualitative market trends relevant to ADF. No invented statistics.

## Background

Trends below are directional observations used for strategy — not measured market
sizes. They must not be quoted as proprietary data.

## Current Situation

AI-assisted development is entering mainstream engineering practice while
organizations still lack shared operating standards for documentation, context, and
governance.

## Analysis

Each trend creates both demand and noise. ADF should attach to durable structural
trends, not short-lived demo fashion.

## Strategy

| Trend | Implication for ADF |
|-------|---------------------|
| **AI adoption** | More teams use AI in coding; need shifts from “can we generate?” to “can we continue and control?” |
| **Developer productivity** | Buyers seek leverage; ADF’s value is continuity and reduced rework, not raw token speed |
| **Knowledge management** | Knowledge scattered across chats and wikis; ADF ties knowledge to architecture and project state |
| **Governance** | Enterprises and institutions ask who approved what; RC1 foundations support this direction; hardening continues |
| **Documentation** | Docs-as-code and living contracts regain importance when AI participates in delivery |
| **Platform engineering** | Internal platforms standardize paths; ADF can sit as AI-engineering framework alongside IDP practices |

## Recommendations

1. Use trends as conversation frames, never as fake percentages.
2. Prefer productivity-via-continuity messaging over “10x” claims.
3. Align Enterprise messaging to governance/documentation trends with RC1 honesty.

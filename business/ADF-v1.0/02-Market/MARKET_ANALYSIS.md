# Market Analysis

## Objective

Establish the market SSOT for ADF: who the product serves, under what conditions,
and how category dynamics affect adoption — without market-size invention.

## Background

ADF `1.0.0-rc1` is an AI Development Framework with Studio, distribution surfaces,
collaboration, planning orchestration, and governance foundations. Product Strategy
defines Community, Professional, Enterprise, and future Cloud editions.

## Current Situation

Organizations are adopting AI coding tools while struggling with context loss, weak
documentation continuity, and unclear governance. ADF addresses structured
AI-assisted engineering, not chat-first assistance as a primary identity.

## Analysis

Market fit is strongest where buyers already feel fragmentation across assistants,
wikis, tickets, and pipelines — and need a shared operating layer for humans and AI
participants. Fit is weaker where the only need is faster autocomplete.

Priority commercial rungs (from Product Strategy): Professional (software houses,
consultants, teams) and Enterprise (governance, audit, deployment, support), with
Community as a useful evaluation and learning path.

## Strategy

1. Treat this package as market SSOT for BUSINESS-005 onward.
2. Segment outreach by buyer maturity and edition fit.
3. Compete on category logic (framework/platform), not assistant feature parity.
4. Keep claims RC1-honest; label roadmap items.

## Recommendations

1. Use ICP and personas to qualify opportunities before feature demos.
2. Pair pain points with adoption barriers in sales discovery.
3. Revisit assumptions after real pilot feedback — do not invent metrics here.

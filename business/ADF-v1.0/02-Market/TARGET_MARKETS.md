# Target Markets

## Objective

Define the primary market arenas ADF should pursue, aligned to editions.

## Background

Product Strategy positions Community for learning/evaluation, Professional for
delivery businesses, Enterprise for organizational control, and Cloud as future only.

## Current Situation

ADF is at RC1. Near-term markets must accept a release-candidate maturity stage and
self-managed deployment — not a hosted Cloud product.

## Analysis

Targeting every segment equally dilutes GTM. Markets should be ranked by problem
fit (continuity, documentation, governance) and willingness to adopt a framework
rather than a chat plugin.

## Strategy

| Priority | Market arena | Primary edition path | Why |
|----------|--------------|----------------------|-----|
| P1 | Software delivery organizations (software houses, consultants, SI) | Professional → Enterprise | Commercial use + repeatable delivery |
| P1 | Mid-size / enterprise engineering orgs adopting AI at scale | Enterprise (eval via Community/Pilot) | Governance and continuity needs |
| P2 | Startups with architecture discipline | Community → Professional | Speed with structure |
| P2 | Government / institutional IT (selective) | Enterprise-oriented | Accountability and documentation posture |
| P3 | Education and individual developers | Community | Learning, research, non-commercial |
| Future | Managed/hosted buyers | Cloud (vision) | Not available at RC1 |

Geographic focus is not locked in this package; pursue markets where English/docs
and self-managed deployment are acceptable unless later BD decisions localize.

## Recommendations

1. Do not lead GTM with Cloud.
2. Use Community as trustworthy evaluation, not bait-and-switch.
3. Qualify government deals for process maturity and RC1 honesty.

## Implementation Plan

Sales and Marketing phases map campaigns to P1 arenas first.

## Deliverables

Priority market table for GTM planning.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Over-focus on individuals only | Keep P1 on commercial delivery orgs |

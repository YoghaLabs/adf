# Customer Segments

## Objective

Define customer segments ADF may serve and how they relate to editions.

## Background

Product Strategy and Target Markets require a shared segment vocabulary for GTM.

## Current Situation

ADF RC1 can be evaluated broadly; commercial paths differ by segment need and
license posture (`/legal` Community; commercial permission for business use).

## Analysis

Segments differ by buying power, process maturity, and whether they need governance
or only individual productivity.

## Strategy

| Segment | Typical need | Edition path | Notes |
|---------|--------------|--------------|-------|
| **Individual Developers** | Personal structure for AI-assisted work | Community | Non-commercial / evaluation |
| **Freelancers** | Continuity across client projects | Community → Professional if commercial | License posture matters |
| **Software Houses** | Repeatable delivery with AI participants | Professional | P1 commercial |
| **Startups** | Speed with architectural discipline | Community → Professional | Fit if process-minded |
| **Mid-size Companies** | Team continuity and standards | Professional → Enterprise | Often hybrid needs |
| **Enterprise** | Governance, audit, deployment, support | Enterprise | Honest RC1 vs roadmap |
| **Government** | Accountability, documentation, control | Enterprise-oriented | Longer cycles |
| **Educational Institutions** | Teaching, research, labs | Community | Non-commercial aligned |
| **System Integrators** | Client delivery frameworks and packages | Professional → Enterprise | Partner-like motions |

## Recommendations

1. Prioritize Software Houses, System Integrators, Mid-size, and Enterprise for commercial GTM.
2. Keep Education and Individuals on Community without forced upgrade pressure.
3. Qualify Freelancers for commercial license needs early.

## Implementation Plan

Personas and ICP refine who inside each segment decides.

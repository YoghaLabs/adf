# Adoption Barriers

## Objective

Identify barriers that slow or block ADF adoption and how to address them honestly.

## Background

Even with problem fit, frameworks face adoption friction: learning cost, toolchain
inertia, license clarity, and maturity perception.

## Current Situation

ADF is `1.0.0-rc1`. Lifecycle stage itself is a barrier if miscommunicated as GA.
Cloud absence is a barrier for hosted-only buyers.

## Analysis

Barriers are not failures of messaging alone; some are structural and should be
acknowledged in diligence.

## Strategy

| Barrier | Why it appears | Response |
|---------|----------------|----------|
| Learning curve | New operating model beyond chat plugins | Guided docs, templates, staged pilot |
| Toolchain inertia | Teams already use assistants + PM + CI | Position as complement; integration via SDK/process |
| Category confusion | Buyers compare on chat quality | Re-frame to framework/platform criteria |
| RC vs GA perception | RC1 not GA | State lifecycle clearly; outline GA gates |
| Commercial license clarity | Community ≠ commercial use | Point to `/legal`; Professional path |
| Enterprise control expectations | Desire for live SSO/IdP now | Foundations exist; production IdP wiring out of RC1 — roadmap honesty |
| Autonomy expectations | Desire for unsupervised agents | Out of RC1; planning orchestration only |
| Cloud-only preference | Want managed service | Cloud = future vision; offer self-managed path or defer |
| Change management | Process change required | Start with one team; Engineering Manager persona |

## Recommendations

1. Surface barriers early in discovery; do not hide RC1 limits.
2. Use Community pilots to reduce learning-curve risk before commercial close.
3. Disqualify Cloud-only mandatory deals for now.

## Implementation Plan

Sales enablement includes barrier talk-tracks; Product roadmap addresses structural gaps over time.

## Deliverables

Barrier register for GTM realism.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Sales overrides barriers with hype | BD-007 + this register |

## Success Metrics

Lost-deal reasons include honest barriers, not surprise after purchase.

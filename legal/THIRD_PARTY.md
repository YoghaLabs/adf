# Third-Party Notices

ADF may include or depend on third-party open-source and proprietary components.

## Principle

**Third-party dependencies retain their original licenses.**

Nothing in the ADF Community License, EULA, or copyright notices is intended to
re-license third-party software contrary to its upstream terms.

## Responsibility

- Upstream copyright holders retain their rights.
- License texts and notices required by those dependencies must be preserved
  where redistribution requires it.
- Operators packaging ADF for distribution should include dependency license
  inventories appropriate to their release artifacts.

## Discovery

Dependency license details are typically found in:

- package manifests (for example `package.json`, `pyproject.toml`)
- upstream project repositories and LICENSE files
- generated SBOM or notice files produced for a specific release build

## ADF Original Work

ADF original architecture, documentation, contracts, and first-party code remain
subject to `COPYRIGHT.md`, `LICENSE.md`, and `EULA.md`.

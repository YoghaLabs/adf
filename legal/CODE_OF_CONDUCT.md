# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to a professional, respectful, and inclusive community around
ADF. Contributors, users, and maintainers should collaborate without harassment,
discrimination, or bad-faith disruption.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment:

- demonstrating empathy and respect
- accepting constructive feedback
- focusing on what is best for the community and the project
- giving credit where due

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, insults, or discriminatory remarks
- publishing others’ private information without permission
- trolling, personal attacks, or sustained disruption
- sexualized language or imagery in project spaces
- any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of
Conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions that are not aligned
with this Code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for
behaviors they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code applies within project spaces (repositories, issues, discussions, and
official communication channels) and when an individual is officially
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the project founder / copyright holder. All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted for ADF as a professional open-source community
standard for respectful collaboration.

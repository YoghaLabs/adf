# Contributing to ADF

Thank you for your interest in ADF (AI Development Framework).

## Before You Contribute

1. Read `COPYRIGHT.md`, `LICENSE.md`, and `EULA.md`.
2. Follow repository architecture locks and documentation SSOT practices.
3. Prefer small, reviewable changes with clear intent.

## Contribution Policy

**All contributions become part of the ADF project under its contribution
policy.**

By submitting a contribution (code, documentation, templates, prompts, designs,
or other materials), you agree that:

1. You have the right to submit the contribution.
2. You grant Yogha Prayoto and the ADF project the rights needed to use,
   modify, distribute, and sublicense the contribution as part of ADF.
3. Your contribution may be licensed under the ADF Community License and any
   future commercial terms chosen by the copyright holder for ADF distributions.
4. You will not submit materials that knowingly infringe third-party rights.

## Standards

- No placeholders or empty documentation in required deliverables
- Do not invent new top-level architecture unless explicitly authorized
- Keep Studio presentation-only; do not move business logic into the UI layer
- Update relevant SSOT files when identity or release state changes

## Conduct

All participants must follow `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`.

## Questions

For licensing or contribution ownership questions, contact the copyright holder.

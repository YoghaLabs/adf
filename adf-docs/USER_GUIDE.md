# User Guide

**ADF v1.0.0-rc1**

## What you get

ADF Studio is the **Control Center** for workspaces, projects, sessions, marketplace, visual intelligence, runtime observability, collaboration, orchestration planning, and enterprise governance views.

## Typical flow

1. Open / switch workspace
2. Browse projects & sessions
3. Collaborate with human + AI participants
4. Plan orchestration pipelines (no auto-run in v1)
5. Review governance, audit, and compliance panels (enterprise)
